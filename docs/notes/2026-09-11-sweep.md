## Bets (two sentences each)

- **Confidence as a first-class layer.** Every verdict says "pattern only" /
  "N readings over D days"; the chart already speaks this way, extend it up
  into the hero and cards. Turns the biggest trust gap into the honesty signal.
- **Sellout signal before more price sources.** Ticketmaster
  `dates.status.code` as "still on sale at face / primary sold out" on every
  event, gating wait vs buy. Supply depth predicts direction better than price.
- **A public track record.** Log every tracked event to showtime and show
  "our buy-by price vs day-of" per event and a rolling hit rate on the
  landing page. Nobody has published the concert day-of curve.
- **Email at the moment of tracking**, alerting on the predicted bottom, not
  just a user target. Converts the one action a visitor takes into a return
  path.
- **One URL per event built for search** ("is it cheaper to wait for X at
  Y?"). Cheapest acquisition for a zero-traffic site; reuses everything built.
